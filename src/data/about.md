I'm a software engineer working across data engineering, machine learning and AI. Most of my work sits where data becomes a product: the pipelines that move it, the models that learn from it, and the systems that keep both reliable once real users depend on them.

I care about the unglamorous parts: idempotent jobs, honest metrics, and models that are cheap to retrain and easy to roll back. I'd rather ship something small that holds than something impressive that needs a babysitter.

The name _fudō-ryū_ (不動流) translates roughly as "the immovable school". It's what I build under, and the standard I try to hold the work to.
